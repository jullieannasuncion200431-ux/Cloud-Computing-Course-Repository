# Mission Reflection — Checkpoint 8

Completing this Multi-Cloud Explorer mission gave me a clearer picture of how AWS, Azure, and GCP differ. Of the three, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most because of its strengths in AI/ML and Kubernetes. Learning that Google created Kubernetes helped me understand why GCP is strong in containerized applications.

Researching all three platforms also showed me their similarities. Each provider offers basic cloud services such as virtual machines, object storage, identity and access management, and managed databases. Although the services have different names, they perform similar functions. Understanding these similarities made it easier for me to compare the three cloud platforms.

The client recommendation activities taught me that business requirements are important when selecting a cloud provider. A startup, university, AI research company, and global e-commerce company can have different priorities. Factors such as budget, existing technology, scalability, and technical requirements should be considered before choosing a cloud platform.

I also gained hands-on experience with basic Linux system administration through the KillerCoda playground. I used commands such as `uname -a`, `lscpu`, `free -h`, and `df -h` to check the server's operating system, CPU, memory, and disk space. This helped me understand how server specifications relate to cloud computing resources.

Overall, this mission improved my knowledge of AWS, Azure, and GCP and helped me understand how to compare cloud platforms based on real business needs. It also improved my GitHub portfolio by adding a documented cloud research and recommendation project.
